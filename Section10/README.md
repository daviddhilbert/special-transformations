# Section 10: Categories with volume forms

The legacy proof snapshots and scaffold sit alongside the active
`Section10` Lake target.

The approved scope for Section 10 is the finite-volume category `RV[n]`
and its lifts into `VF[n]`. Both RV volume maps are finite-to-one. This
scope applies to both RV-valued and Gamma-valued volume forms, including
the forward lifting results in Theorem 10.5 and Corollary 10.6. The
broader ambient categories can supply earlier lemmas as needed. The
Section 9 dependencies and the prescribed-Jacobian lifting strategy are
recorded in the [Section 9 handoff](../Section9/HANDOFF.md).

Do not bulk-import these legacy snapshots into the active library. Audit
their paper statements, parameters, explicit hypotheses, placeholders and
actual dependencies before reuse. Old headers describe historical states.

See `../Archive/MOVE-INDEX.json` for old paths and initial content hashes.

The active library includes Remark 10.2, Theorems 10.4 and 10.5, and
Corollary 10.6 for the finite-volume RV and Gamma categories. It includes
the constant-volume subcategories and the unmeasured degree-zero cases.
See [status](STATUS.md), [handoff](HANDOFF.md), and the
[reuse survey](REUSE-SURVEY.md).
