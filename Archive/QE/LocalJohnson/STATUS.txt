    The general criterion: prime models, amalgamation, and one-variable

    hasQEb_ACVF_val. The latter includes formulas with bound variables in scope.

Mathematical scope:
  Ordinary, one-sorted algebraically closed nontrivially valued fields, in
  the ring language with the binary relation v(x) <= v(y), using Mathlib's
  multiplicative valuation convention. All field and residue characteristics
  are allowed. The auxiliary theory valField also permits trivial valuations.
  The nontriviality axiom says there is x != 0 with v(x) < 1, a nonzero element
  of the maximal ideal. Inverting x gives an element outside the valuation ring.
  QE means a single quantifier-free formula uniform over all models and
  assignments, with arbitrary parameter-variable types in the stated universe.
