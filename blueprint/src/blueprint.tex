\section{Paper}
Yimu Yin, \emph{Special transformations in algebraically closed valued fields},
Annals of Pure and Applied Logic (2010), arXiv:1006.2467.

\section{Formalization target}
Formalize the two steps of the Hrushovski--Kazhdan construction presented in
the paper:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item every definable subset admits a special bijection to an RV-pullback
        (Theorem 5.5, Corollary 5.6);
  \item isomorphic RV-objects have isomorphic lifts (Theorem 7.6,
        Corollary 7.7),
\end{enumerate}
and the volume-form refinements of Section 10 (Theorems 10.4, 10.5,
Corollary 10.6).
