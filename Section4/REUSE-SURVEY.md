## Mathlib source inspection

The pinned `Mathlib/CategoryTheory` contains 1,089 Lean source files. Relevant
modules include `Category/Basic`, `Functor/FullyFaithful`,
`ObjectProperty/FullSubcategory`, `Grothendieck`,
`Bicategory/Grothendieck`, and `FiberedCategory/Grothendieck`.
The categorical construction for a functor into `Cat` is already available;
it is distinct from the scissor quotient needed here.

`GroupTheory/MonoidLocalization/GrothendieckGroup.lean` supplies
`Algebra.GrothendieckGroup` and its additive counterpart, with the universal
property. Injectivity of the canonical map requires cancellation; Section 4
does not supply that hypothesis in general.

Other inspected candidates are `RingTheory/Congruence/Defs.lean`
(`RingCon`, `ringConGen`) and
`RingTheory/AlgebraicIndependent/TranscendenceBasis.lean`
(`IsTranscendenceBasis.cardinalMk_eq_trdeg`,
`Algebra.IsAlgebraic.trdeg_le_cardinalMk`, and the algebraic-independence
matroid). The latter generator bound is already in the pin. Its field-adjoin
application still needs its precise bridge. No direct general
Krull-dimension/transcendence-degree bridge was found in the limited local
searches; inspect Noether normalization and the algebraic-geometry dimension
APIs before rebuilding it.

For the dimension comparison, a focused inspection also found
`Algebra.trdeg_le_of_injective`,
`IntermediateField.isAlgebraic_adjoin_iff_top`, and the scoped
`IntermediateField.algebraAdjoinAdjoin` algebraicity instance. These allow
a small bridge from subfields generated by finite VF tuples to the existing
`Algebra.IsAlgebraic.trdeg_le_cardinalMk` bound. The chosen route reuses these
pinned Mathlib results and the accepted Section 3 `relativeTrdeg` API;
it does not rebuild transcendence-basis or generator-bound theory.

For the open-polydisc stage, `Mathlib/Algebra/MvPolynomial/Funext.lean`
already supplies `MvPolynomial.funext_set`: a polynomial vanishing on a
box with infinite sides is zero. Finite polynomial avoidance follows by
taking a nonzero finite product. `Section3/RingSyntax.lean` supplies the
released-language translation and interpretation of generic ring terms;
its existing `FreeCommRing` bridge can express a finite polynomial with
named coefficients without a competing language. The univariate
`PolynomialSyntax`, `RVTermLocal`, `PolynomialLocal`, and `QFLocal` are
local proof references for the finite-tuple extension. Their earlier
verification is not a prerequisite here. This route is still under
consideration, not a completed general polydisc theorem.

with `Krull dimension`, `trdeg Krull`, and `Noether normalization`.

  `2e9f92dc5fbeacd17189dcf0cdb499f528dfd77c`, has

  Its separate affine dimension-formula source is adapted from Anthropic
  FLT and is substantially larger. That alternative was inspected as a

## O-minimality follow-up for 4.11

Focused searches of the pinned Mathlib model-theory sources did not find
an o-minimality or DOAG quantifier-elimination development. The public

`Sundogcert/OMinimalStructure.lean` defines an o-minimal set calculus on
`Fin n → ℝ`, with every real singleton included among the definable atoms.
`OMinimalPointFns.lean` supplies definable nth-point functions for finite
fibres on that interface. This does not provide the needed theorem over
an arbitrary, possibly non-Archimedean value group with the original

not replace the earlier genuine saturation arguments. No port or checked
input is taken from it. The inspected source, Apache-2.0 license and pins
are retained in the scratch survey; it uses Lean/Mathlib 4.30.0.

The paper's standing hypothesis immediately before Convention 2.5 is that
Gamma(S) is nontrivial. Keep its named nonzero value explicit in the 4.11
definable-point step; arbitrary o-minimality without a named nonzero value
does not supply that step over the original base.

For the uniform infinite-value-fibre step, no o-minimality port or uniform
ball-complexity hypothesis is needed. The accepted Section 3 angular-pattern
theorem gives finite/cofinite fibres inside each fixed non-infinity value
class. Applying genuine saturation to simultaneous counts of a set and
its complement gives a uniform bound for the finite fibres; infinity is
then defined by a fixed first-order count. This new Section 4 argument
passed its combined compiler check and all nine complete headline audits.

The complete 4.11 argument reuses the given Section 3
`gamma_definable_finite_thresholds`, `algebraic_rv_gamma_base_iff`, and
`lemma_3_6_rv` interfaces. The new endpoint calculus is over the actual
ordered value group, with complete raw preimages and original-base
definability. Minimal finite controllers are unique and definable; their
individual endpoints are definable by ordered selection. Products,
inverses, unique square roots, and the standing named nontrivial value
give a definable sample in every relevant order cell. Iteration over RV
rows then proves the actual maximal-fibre theorem. The combined check
passed compiler exit zero and all 98 full headline/axiom checks; full types
were inspected. Separate module builds and integration remain.

## Residue-field comparison follow-up for 4.10

`MaximalRVExtension.exists_field_representing_graph` and

Focused local source searches found no checked residue stable-embedding

For a mathematical reference, David Marker's
[valued-field notes, Section 4](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~marker/math512-f18/valued_fields_4.pdf)
give pure residue-field definability in Corollary 4.25 and stable
embeddedness in Corollary 4.27; Exercise 4.28 concerns algebraicity over
residue parameters. These notes are proof references only at this point.
They have not been silently added to the authorization for results in
Marker's *Model Theory: An Introduction* or to the checked input list.
Finite/cofinite unary fibres alone are not substituted for the needed
all-arity field-definability and algebraicity bridges.

The new `ResiduePurity` syntax chain now derives the all-arity pure-field
pullback theorem from the released sorted QE, rather than adding a stable
embedding hypothesis. A term on fixed-coefficient residue inputs has one
fixed RV coefficient times a pure ring-language definable residue function.
The total residue operations contribute definable zero-test branches;
equality, the RV preorder, and the unit predicate then reduce to pure field
predicates. The actual residue subfield named by a real substructure and
the coefficient closure needed for parameter control are also constructed.
Eight modules passed compiler exit zero and all 73 complete headline/axiom
checks, with full types inspected. Only the three permitted axioms occur.
The exact-base algebraicity and Krull-dimension comparison remain separate
obligations, so this does not yet establish Lemma 4.10.

cocartesian monoidal, and cartesian product APIs for the generic laws.
`DefinableUnionCoproduct.lean` supplies the genuine disjoint-union universal
property; the VF modules prove their geometric scissors and distributivity
isomorphisms. For finite RV fibres, the actual positive quotient injects
into the ambient quotient by full-subcategory isomorphism lifting, and the

new modules passed a combined compiler run and all 84 full headline/axiom
