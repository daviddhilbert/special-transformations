## Status

**The criterion now produces quantifier elimination.** `QE/ACF_HasQE.lean` proves

```

HasQE_acf_frozen : ∀ (α : Type) (φ : Language.ring.Formula α),
```

Johnson's Lemma 2.1.4, with all three of his hypotheses discharged at ACF: prime models over
arbitrary substructures (`QE/PrimeModels_full.lean`: the fraction field of
`FreeCommRing S ⧸ ker (FreeCommRing.lift e)`, universal by `IsFractionRing.lift`), amalgamation
(`amalgamation_field`, tensor products of fields) and 1-ec (`oneEC_field_acf`). What was missing
was the "last mile" `H` — quantifier-free-existential determinism from `qfDetermined` — which is
`QE/H_of_qfDetermined.lean` (`termSub`/`termSubMap`/`termSubEmb`, `exists_qf_collapse`), fed to
the compactness join `hasQE_of_qfDet`. This is the first theory for which the criterion has been
run end to end syntactically.

Nothing here yet discharges the `sorry` at `theorem_2_3_quantifier_elimination` in
`Milestone0/Encoding.lean`: that statement is in the frozen tagged `L_RV` language, and the
`L_val → L_RV` transfer (Yin's §3) is a separate obligation. Two *other* reductions exist, each
conditional on named `Prop`s, with no axiom added; Weispfenning's theorem is never assumed as an

## Duplication and coherence

- `Lemma37_transcendental.lean` and `Lifting_transcendental.lean` are **independent**
  formalisations of overlapping lemmas (one over `Valuation` with an `Algebra` frame, the other
  over a `Subfield`), and neither imports the other. Agreement between them is stronger evidence
  than either alone.
- `Reduction_Lv.lean` and `Reduction_assembly.lean` are two reductions with different hypothesis
  sets. They have **not** been compared, and `VFGenerated ↔ IsVFGen` is an unproved coherence
  obligation between them.
- `Johnson23_valuation.lean` (K ⊆ L part) and `Johnson23_subfield.lean` are **independent
  formalisations of Lemmas 2.3.3 and 2.3.8** in different frameworks (single `Γ₀` with a ring
  hom, versus a `Subfield` with two value groups). Neither imports the other; the agreement is
  corroboration. The subfield version is the one whose shape matches `Language.val`; the
  single-Γ version is the one that composes with the ball combinatorics of 2.3.2 in the same
  file.
